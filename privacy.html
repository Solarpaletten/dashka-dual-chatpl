Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Dashka Translator Lite collects the following information:

  • Voice recordings: Temporary audio data captured through your device's microphone for speech-to-text translation. Audio is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.
  • Translation history: Your translation history is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous app performance data to improve user experience. No personal identifiers are collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

Voice data is transmitted to third-party speech recognition services (Google Cloud Speech-to-Text API) solely for the purpose of real-time translation. We do not:

  • Retain voice recordings after processing
  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Use data for advertising purposes

3. Data Storage & Security

All personal data (translation history, user preferences) is stored locally on your device using secure Android storage mechanisms. We do not operate backend servers for user data storage.

4. Third-Party Services

Dashka Translator Lite uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Translate API — for text translation
  • Google Text-to-Speech API — for voice output
  • Google Cloud Speech-to-Text API — for voice input

These services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

RECORD_AUDIO: This permission is required to enable voice input functionality. Audio is captured only when you actively press the microphone button and is processed in real-time. No audio is stored or retained.

6. Children's Privacy

Dashka Translator Lite is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your local translation history at any time
  • Disable voice input features
  • Request information about data we hold about you

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
